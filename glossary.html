Glossary

109 terms in plain English. Each one links to the lesson section where it's explained in full.

ABCDEFGHIKLMNOPRSTUV

A

Accuracy
Fraction of predictions that are correct: (TP + TN) / total. Misleading on imbalanced data. Lesson 9 →
Activation function
The non-linear function applied after a neuron's weighted sum, e.g. ReLU, sigmoid, tanh, softmax. Lesson 18 →
AdaBoost
Boosting method that re-weights training examples so each new weak learner focuses on previous mistakes. Lesson 14 →
Adam
A gradient-descent optimiser that combines momentum with a per-parameter adaptive step size. Lesson 19 →
Attention
Mechanism where each token computes a softmax-weighted average of other tokens' values, using query–key similarity. Lesson 19 →
AUC (ROC-AUC)
Area under the ROC curve: the probability a random positive is scored above a random negative. 0.5 = random, 1 = perfect. Lesson 9 →

B

Backpropagation
Efficient computation of all gradients in a neural network by applying the chain rule backwards from the output. Lesson 18 →
Bagging
Bootstrap aggregating: train models on bootstrap samples and average their predictions to reduce variance. Lesson 14 →
Batch / mini-batch
The group of examples used to compute one gradient update. Lesson 7 →
Bayes' theorem
P(A | B) = P(B | A) P(A) / P(B): updates a prior belief with evidence into a posterior. Lesson 4 →
Bias (error)
Error from wrong assumptions; high bias causes underfitting. Lesson 10 →
Bias (parameter)
The intercept term b added to a weighted sum. Lesson 6 →
Bootstrap sample
A sample of n examples drawn with replacement from n originals (about 63% distinct). Lesson 14 →

C

Centroid
The mean point of a cluster in k-means. Lesson 16 →
Chain rule
Derivative of a composition: d/dx f(g(x)) = f′(g(x)) g′(x). Lesson 3 →
Classification
Supervised learning where the label is a category. Lesson 1 →
Clustering
Grouping unlabelled examples by similarity. Lesson 16 →
Confusion matrix
Table of TP, FP, FN and TN counts (or a K×K table for K classes). Lesson 9 →
Convolution
Sliding a small kernel over an input and summing element-wise products at each position. Lesson 19 →
Covariance
Average product of two features' deviations from their means; positive if they rise together. Lesson 17 →
Cross-validation (k-fold)
Split training data into k folds; train on k−1 and validate on the remaining one, k times; average the scores. Lesson 5 →
Curse of dimensionality
In many dimensions data becomes sparse and distances lose meaning. Lesson 11 →

D

Data leakage
Information from validation/test data (or the future) sneaking into training, inflating scores. Lesson 5 →
DBSCAN
Density-based clustering that grows clusters from core points and labels isolated points as noise. Lesson 16 →
Decision boundary
The surface where a classifier switches its prediction from one class to another. Lesson 8 →
Decision tree
Model that predicts by asking a sequence of feature-threshold questions. Lesson 13 →
Demographic parity
Fairness criterion: every group is selected (approved, flagged) at the same rate. Summarised by the ratio of the lower to the higher selection rate. Lesson 21 →
Derivative
Slope of a function at a point; how fast the output changes as the input changes. Lesson 3 →
Dimensionality reduction
Compressing many features into fewer while keeping most information. Lesson 17 →
Dot product
Sum of products of matching components: a·b = Σ aᵢbᵢ. Lesson 2 →
Dropout
Randomly switching off neurons during training to reduce overfitting. Lesson 19 →

E

Eigenvector / eigenvalue
A direction v that a matrix only stretches: Σv = λv; λ is the stretch factor. Lesson 17 →
Ensemble
A combination of several models whose predictions are pooled. Lesson 14 →
Entropy
Impurity measure −Σ p log₂ p; 0 for a pure node. Lesson 13 →
Epoch
One full pass over the training data. Lesson 7 →
Equal opportunity
Fairness criterion: the true-positive rate is the same in every group, so qualified people are approved equally often. Lesson 21 →
Equalised odds
Fairness criterion: both the true-positive rate and the false-positive rate are equal across groups. Lesson 21 →

F

F1 score
Harmonic mean of precision and recall: 2TP / (2TP + FP + FN). Lesson 9 →
Feature
An input variable describing an example (x). Lesson 1 →
Feature engineering
Creating and transforming features to make patterns easier to learn. Lesson 20 →

G

Generalization
How well a model performs on new, unseen data. Lesson 5 →
Gini impurity
Impurity measure 1 − Σ p²; 0 for a pure node. Lesson 13 →
Gradient
Vector of all partial derivatives; points in the direction of steepest increase. Lesson 3 →
Gradient boosting
Boosting that fits each new tree to the residuals (negative gradient) of the current ensemble. Lesson 14 →
Gradient descent
Optimisation by repeatedly stepping against the gradient: w ← w − η∇L. Lesson 7 →

H

Hinge loss
max(0, 1 − y·score); zero for points correctly outside the SVM margin. Lesson 15 →
Hyperparameter
A setting chosen before training (k, λ, learning rate, depth), usually by cross-validation. Lesson 10 →

I

Imputation
Filling in missing values, e.g. with the training median. Lesson 20 →
Inertia
k-means objective: sum of squared distances from points to their nearest centroid. Lesson 16 →

K

k-means
Clustering algorithm alternating 'assign to nearest centroid' and 'move centroid to mean'. Lesson 16 →
k-nearest neighbours
Predicts from the majority (or average) of the k most similar training examples. Lesson 11 →
Kernel (CNN)
A small learned grid of weights slid over an image; also called a filter. Lesson 19 →
Kernel trick (SVM)
Computing dot products in a high-dimensional feature space directly from the original vectors. Lesson 15 →

L

L1 / Lasso
Penalty λΣ|w|; sets many weights to exactly zero. Lesson 10 →
L2 / Ridge
Penalty λΣw²; shrinks all weights smoothly (weight decay). Lesson 10 →
Label
The target value to predict (y). Lesson 1 →
Laplace smoothing
Adding 1 to every count so no probability is ever zero. Lesson 12 →
Learning rate
Step size η in gradient descent. Lesson 7 →
Log loss (cross-entropy)
−[y ln p + (1−y) ln(1−p)]; punishes confident wrong probabilities heavily. Lesson 8 →
Logistic regression
Linear classifier that outputs p = σ(w·x + b). Lesson 8 →
Loss function
A number measuring how wrong a model's predictions are; training minimises it. Lesson 1 →
LSTM
Recurrent network with gates and a cell state that remember over long sequences. Lesson 19 →

M

Margin (SVM)
Width of the gap between the classes around the boundary: 2/‖w‖. Lesson 15 →
Matrix
A rectangular table of numbers; a dataset X has one row per example. Lesson 2 →
Mean squared error (MSE)
Average of squared errors: (1/n) Σ (ŷᵢ − yᵢ)². Lesson 6 →
Model card
A short document that records a model's intended use, how it was evaluated (including per group) and its known limitations. Lesson 21 →

N

Naive Bayes
Classifier using Bayes' theorem with features assumed independent given the class. Lesson 12 →
Neural network (MLP)
Layers of neurons, each computing an activation of a weighted sum. Lesson 18 →
Norm
Length of a vector; Euclidean (L2) √Σv², Manhattan (L1) Σ|v|. Lesson 2 →
Normal distribution
The bell curve, set by mean μ and standard deviation σ; 68/95/99.7% within 1/2/3σ. Lesson 4 →
Normal equation
Closed-form least-squares solution w = (XᵀX)⁻¹Xᵀy. Lesson 6 →

O

One-hot encoding
Turning a category into 0/1 columns, one per category. Lesson 20 →
Overfitting
Fitting noise in the training data: low training error, high test error. Lesson 5 →

P

Parameter
A number inside the model learned during training (weights, biases). Lesson 1 →
PCA
Principal component analysis: projects data onto the directions of greatest variance. Lesson 17 →
Pipeline
A chain of preprocessing steps and a model, fitted together on training data. Lesson 20 →
Pooling
Downsampling a feature map, e.g. keeping the max of each 2×2 window. Lesson 19 →
Precision
Of predicted positives, the fraction truly positive: TP / (TP + FP). Lesson 9 →
Prior / posterior
Belief before evidence (prior) and after updating with Bayes' theorem (posterior). Lesson 4 →
Proxy feature
A feature that stands in for another, often sensitive, attribute because it is correlated with it — e.g. a postcode standing in for ethnicity. Lesson 21 →

R

Random forest
Bagged decision trees that each consider a random subset of features at every split. Lesson 14 →
Recall (sensitivity)
Of actual positives, the fraction found: TP / (TP + FN). Lesson 9 →
Regression
Supervised learning where the label is a number. Lesson 1 →
Regularization
Adding a penalty on model complexity to reduce overfitting. Lesson 10 →
ReLU
Activation max(0, z); default for hidden layers. Lesson 18 →
Residual
The difference between a true value and the prediction. Lesson 6 →
RMSE / MAE
Root mean squared error (punishes big errors) and mean absolute error. Lesson 9 →
ROC curve
True positive rate vs false positive rate across all thresholds. Lesson 9 →
R² (coefficient of determination)
1 − SS_res / SS_tot: fraction of variance explained compared with predicting the mean. Lesson 6 →

S

Sigmoid
σ(z) = 1/(1 + e^(−z)); squashes any number into (0, 1). Lesson 8 →
Silhouette score
(b − a)/max(a, b) averaged over points; higher means tighter, better-separated clusters. Lesson 16 →
Softmax
Turns a vector of scores into probabilities summing to 1: e^(zₖ) / Σ e^(zⱼ). Lesson 8 →
Standard deviation
Square root of the variance; typical distance from the mean. Lesson 4 →
Standardisation (z-score)
(x − μ)/σ: rescale a feature to mean 0 and standard deviation 1. Lesson 4 →
Stochastic gradient descent (SGD)
Gradient descent using one random example (or a mini-batch) per update. Lesson 7 →
Supervised learning
Learning from examples that have labels. Lesson 1 →
Support vectors
Training points on or inside the SVM margin; they alone determine the boundary. Lesson 15 →
SVM
Support vector machine: a maximum-margin classifier, extended to curved boundaries with kernels. Lesson 15 →

T

t-SNE
Non-linear method for visualising high-dimensional data in 2D; for exploration only. Lesson 17 →
Test set
Data held back until the end for one honest estimate of performance. Lesson 5 →
Threshold
Probability cut-off that turns a score into a class decision. Lesson 9 →
Transformer
Architecture built on self-attention; the basis of modern language models. Lesson 19 →

U

Underfitting
Model too simple to capture the pattern: both training and test error high. Lesson 5 →
Unsupervised learning
Learning structure from data without labels. Lesson 1 →

V

Validation set
Data used to compare models and choose hyperparameters. Lesson 5 →
Vanishing gradients
Gradients shrinking through many layers so early layers barely learn. Lesson 19 →
Variance (error)
Error from sensitivity to the particular training set; high variance causes overfitting. Lesson 10 →
Variance (statistics)
Average squared distance from the mean. Lesson 4 →
Vector
An ordered list of numbers; e.g. one example's features or a model's weights. Lesson 2 →
