End-of-part tests

Five mixed tests, one per part. Find out what has stuck and what to revisit.

How the tests work

Each part ends with a 15-question test mixing all of its lessons. The questions are new — not the ones from the lesson quizzes — and the answer order is shuffled every time. After you submit you get your score, an explanation for every question, and links to the exact sections to re-read. Your best score is saved in this browser.
